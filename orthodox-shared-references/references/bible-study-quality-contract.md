# Bible Study Quality Contract

Use this compact acceptance contract for Orthodox Bible-study guides. It governs existing-guide revision, source packs, readiness audits and authorized new work. The authorized scope, protected Scripture and assigned editorial role still control. Historical precedents and craft references do not create additional promotion checklists.

## Load by need

- Load this before writing guide prose or judging readiness; not for routing or lookup alone.
- [`bible-study-guide-structure-standard.md`](bible-study-guide-structure-standard.md) owns exact headings, passage layout and Teaching Notes. Preserve an existing approved form during bounded revision; structural migration needs scope.
- [`patristic-sourcing-policy.md`](patristic-sourcing-policy.md) and [`lexical-and-translation-depth-standard.md`](lexical-and-translation-depth-standard.md) own their evidence details when those claims are material.
- Verify worship and commemoration claims against identified lectionary, Synaxarium and service-text sources; keep their edition, reference frame and authority explicit.
- [`spiritually-edifying-exegesis-voice-standard.md`](spiritually-edifying-exegesis-voice-standard.md) supplies prose diagnostics and worked depth/revision examples when needed; use the examples for a concrete weakness or brief, not as sentences to paste into every guide. NT engagement guidance and the literary synthesis reference are optional, targeted coaching for a diagnosed weakness or explicit voice request, not independent release authorities.
- [`historical-precedent-use-policy.md`](historical-precedent-use-policy.md) owns selective book/genre knowledge and historical applicability. Resolve book and passage before loading precedents.
- Consult visual or publication standards only when those activities are authorized. A prose-only revision does not authorize acquiring visuals, regenerating media, synchronizing copies or publishing live.

## Revision workflow and comparative acceptance

1. Resolve the exact existing guides, audience, Scripture edition/frame, approved structure and allowed writes. Read the whole guide and relevant book context before choosing changes. Do not expand a bounded revision into unrelated new work.
2. Record a compact delta brief: actual weaknesses, expected reader gains, protected strong lines/book-specific insights, useful evidence and scope boundaries. Reuse checked research honestly; investigate unresolved weight-bearing claims rather than collecting decorative material.
3. Choose retain, targeted correction/deepening, section rewrite or justified rebuild. Revise meaning first, then spiritual consequence, voice and proportion. Start a new guide with the same passage-led work: identify its actual tension or promise, decisive words/argument and warranted response before selecting optional apparatus. Deepening should make a reader notice something defensible in this passage, understand why it matters, and receive or respond to it in Christ. No change, a cut or a qualification may be the best result; preservation cannot excuse falsehood or genuinely weak teaching.
4. Compare the whole candidate and diff with the baseline. Locate representative strong lines, images, scriptural discoveries and safeguards that survive or disappear; give a reason for a material loss rather than saying vaguely that the strengths were preserved. Restore only sound, useful strengths, not every attractive sentence. State concrete gains and whether added length earns its space. Give a substantial insight one explanatory home, with short cross-pointers where needed; do not repeat the same source caveat, Gospel companion or safeguard as documentary padding. Necessary local safety guidance may recur. Recheck adjacent context and the guide's overall argument, not just edited sentences. Reject a grander-sounding version without defensible gain.
5. Freeze the candidate's exact bytes/hash and evidence for bounded independent acceptance. One consolidated verdict and one targeted correction pass normally suffice; report unresolved blockers instead of looping for appearance. Material edits reopen acceptance for affected claims/sections and their cross-guide consequences. Never reuse approval for changed bytes.
6. Replace the accepted guide only when authorized, preserving the prior version and verifying the saved replacement. A skipped revision leaves the accepted guide intact. Keep collateral guide edits, bookkeeping, synchronized copies, media and publication within their own authorized scope.

Keep the delta brief and review evidence in editorial source-pack or audit records, not reader prose. Use the agreed review format and do not require a read-only reviewer to modify files.

## One substantive acceptance rubric

For each row, use **sound as written; specific revision needed; unresolved evidence; not applicable**. Every adverse verdict names the sentence/verse, evidence and smallest useful repair. Do not sum scores or require a change in every row.

| Test | Inspect and demonstrate |
|---|---|
| Close Scripture | Approved wording, speaker, sense-unit, immediate context, argument, literary form and adopted numbering remain correct. Explain decisive or difficult clauses, a contrast, verb, repeated phrase, connector or change of address when it changes the reading; do not merely paraphrase every verse or skip its difficulty. |
| Truth and Orthodox precision | Historical, lexical, liturgical, doctrinal and attributed claims have appropriate evidence; direct source, ecclesial reception and editorial inference stay distinguishable. |
| Interpretive depth | Show the connection from observed textual detail to supported meaning to spiritual consequence in Christ, in natural prose rather than a repeated formula. An intertext or Father should explain something the reader would otherwise miss, not merely add another theme or name. Preserve a strong existing insight instead of burying it under additions. |
| Spiritual and pastoral benefit | Show what this passage gives the reader to receive, lament, confess, praise, endure or do, with a credible path of grace. Keep sin, suffering, temptation, illness and injury distinct; no invented motives or coercive diagnosis. Necessary hard-text safeguards stay close to the risky verse or claim in reader-facing body prose, not only in Teaching Notes or sources; detailed provenance may remain in notes. |
| Engaging prose | Natural explanatory order, concrete words, clear terms, varied rhythm and earned memorable lines help a modern parish reader continue. Each developed paragraph advances understanding, wonder, consolation or a warranted response; atmosphere and slogan stacks are not reader gain. Delight includes recognition, clarity and truthful beauty in lament, not compulsory cheerfulness. No fabricated scenes or imitation of famous writers. |
| Preservation and proportion | Named defects are repaired; distinctive strengths, source fidelity and approved form survive. Added quotes, language notes, tables or paragraphs each do useful work; repetition may be cut and no-change may pass. |

Review evidence must be **specific enough to check and concise enough to use**. For each required issue, citation or worship claim, name the actual claim/location and the source/context that supports its disposition. Group equivalent findings where appropriate without losing their qualifications. Labels such as `editorial` or `verified` and long boilerplate do not demonstrate a check. A missing disposition is an unresolved coverage gap, not by itself a literary rejection: obtain an evidence-backed resolution and the required review. Never invent a writer or reviewer judgment to fill a row. Do not invent a character-count rule as a proxy for evidence.

Keep **manuscript repair**, **underlying source resolution** and **release checks** distinct. For example, omitting a disputed worship claim can make prose safe while the service identity remains unresolved. Do not label that history resolved. Read the actual footnote, Sources paragraph and metadata separately before saying where a credit appears; preserve printed page, PDF-object index and section as different locators. Unseen checker logs cannot be certified as inherited-only errors: report what was run, what was supplied, and which release gate remains unverified. A bounded manuscript verdict does not certify installation or publication.

**Release blockers:** altered/mismatched Scripture; unsupported quotation or authoritative attribution presented as fact; quotation used against an explicit source/task restriction; doctrinal contradiction; dangerous pastoral misreading; unresolved evidence controlling the interpretation. Beauty and verifier success cannot offset a blocker. Sensitive doctrinal formulations require a qualified Coptic theological reader rather than a model's author list as final authority. Distinguish an approval that is not claimed from an approval that the retained claim or explicit contract requires; a false rights-clearance or human-signoff flag alone must not manufacture a universal new gate. Identify the actual controlling claim and requirement, without inferring permission or clearance from silence.

The guide should help a Coptic Orthodox reader understand the actual passage and live in Christ. Evaluate spiritual consequence across the whole guide, not by making every paragraph perform the same repentance sequence. Argument, lament, joy, consolation, wonder and plain explanation can each be faithful to the text. Explain unfamiliar technical terms where they matter. Speak as a sincere teacher, not a simulated spiritual elder.

## Structure and optional apparatus

The structure standard owns exact syntax. For new or authorized structurally migrated session guides: frontmatter first; no duplicate body H1, Navigation or same-page wikilinks; lead then interleaved H2 passage units with Scripture blockquote and close explanation; exactly one `## Spiritual Fruit 🍇` followed by one `## Teaching Notes`; Sources last when present. Do not force a migration into a narrow correction or explicitly preserved form.

Place useful language, patristic and worship material under the passage it illuminates. A single Gems subsection uses `Gems from the Fathers 💎`; multiple ones have unique meaningful subtitles. Use `The Church's Voice in Worship ⛪` for a substantive verified connection, after the lead only if it governs the whole guide.

Optional geography, characters, language, typology, tables, iconography and scenes earn their space. Keep non-applicability checks in editorial evidence, not empty reader headings or compulsory absence sentences. No quote count, Greek-note quota, scenic ending or fixed fruit count determines readiness. Spiritual Fruit remains concrete and passage-specific; its length and ending follow the passage. Link a mentioned published study with site-absolute Markdown syntax without `.md`, but edit a companion study's return link only if that file is in scope; otherwise propose it.

## Source honesty

### Scripture and text frame

Never type Scripture from memory. Retrieve and compare the approved text, preserving exact words, source punctuation, verse coverage and the actual frame.

- Approved NKJV NT source note: `Scripture is from NKJV.` Keep visible verse numbers, preferably inline `<sup>` markers in blockquoted paragraphs. Clear speech of Jesus uses semantic `<span class="words-of-christ">...</span>`, not raw color/style markup.
- Brenton OT source note: `Embedded Scripture is from Brenton's English Septuagint (LXX).` Explain irregular source shape without inventing verses.
- Reader-facing project Psalm citations are MT-primary with verified labeled LXX counterpart when different, for example `Psalm 68:5 (LXX Ps 67:6)`. Check both chapter and verse in the actual editions; no blanket offset. Preserve a service's printed reference and convention separately instead of silently renumbering its witness.
- Keep local paths, tools, models, checksums, permissions and extraction mechanics out of reader prose. Preserve approved Scripture and authenticated quotations despite house-style punctuation preferences.

### Patristic and other authoritative claims

Prefer opened primary text, exact work/section locator and appropriate source rights. Discovery tools and familiar names do not prove a claim. Distinguish direct comment, close parallel, thematic witness and scoped absence. Explain the witness's actual point; quote verified wording when it adds interpretive force, qualification or spiritual texture beyond the paraphrase. Each direct quotation has a portable semantic Markdown footnote with short citation and direct/deep URL when available, not an intermediate source-list hop.

Verify paraphrases as well as quotes. Uncertainty about a Father is never a reason to say `the Fathers teach`; collective claims require evidence for their scope and may not imply unanimity without support. Otherwise verify, qualify as editorial synthesis, or omit. Distinguish received Coptic doctrinal witness, wider Christian exegetical witness, modern pastoral interpretation and disputed/limited-use witness. Authenticity and suitability for this interpretation are separate checks. Omit a useless Fathers subsection; a fully verified paraphrase may be enough.

### Lexical, liturgical and visual claims

A language note needs **observed textual fact → supported interpretive consequence → gain to this reader**. Verify inflected form, contextual sense and textual witness under the lexical standard. `Checked, no useful addition` is valid. A synonym contrast, name meaning or verbal resemblance alone does not establish doctrine or intended allusion.

Identify the current, authoritative lectionary or Synaxarium source appropriate to the task and record its edition/version and provenance in editorial evidence. Keep the checked source baseline consistent through review. A historical citation or unavailable source does not establish current evidence. Before carrying forward an absence finding, rerun the lookup in every declared reference frame, including source/LXX references where MT mappings are missing. Preserve older absence findings as historical evidence, not current advice. Index presence alone proves neither verse endpoints nor exact selected words. Never infer appointment, saint, feast or commemoration from thematic fit. Distinguish actual placement from resonance, preserve source authority and explain verified spiritual significance. Later remembrance must not become a Gospel fact. State the verified worship fact and its spiritual significance in reader prose; keep detailed provenance in editorial or source notes unless a short qualification is essential to prevent a false claim.

Consult visual-source and iconography guidance when visual theology or an asset is actually in scope. Label icon, manuscript, map, museum object, Western art or generated schematic accurately; keep attribution captions compact and teaching directions in Teaching Notes. Do not require image research to pass a prose-only or visual-excluded review.

## Verification and readiness reporting

Apply the structure standard, this quality contract and exact Scripture/source checks to the actual candidate. Run available automated checks when access and scope permit. Read the saved changed sections and whole-guide flow. Scan active guides rather than audit folders quoting forbidden markers. A mechanically clean artifact can still fail the substantive rubric; a failed checker is evidence to diagnose, not permission for an out-of-scope structural rewrite.

For a voice-sensitive final pass, use the voice reference's ear/read-aloud diagnostic on the lead, a major explanation, Spiritual Fruit and heavily changed prose. Report whether this was an actual read-aloud check or a text-only cadence review; never claim a read-aloud test from silent inspection.

Verify bookkeeping only after accepted guide checks and only in scope. When Markdown materially changes, identify affected derivative documents, print editions or presentations and report their freshness. Do not regenerate assets or rewrite metadata-only workflows by implication. Report **Markdown ready** separately from **derivative artifacts current** and **live published**. Acceptance of prose alone does not authorize publication.
