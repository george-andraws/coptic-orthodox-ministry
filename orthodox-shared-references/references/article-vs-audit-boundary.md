# Article vs Audit Boundary

## Reader-facing article or guide

Contains finished teaching content only: Scripture, explanation, patristic/liturgical depth, spiritual application, lesson-guide material, glossary, and sources.

Do not include shell commands, query counts, local tool output, model names, file sizes, draft notes, methodology meta, TODOs, or unresolved process notes.

## Audit note

Carries process and provenance: exact commands, raw query results, source-status notes, model/reviewer notes, verification evidence, status decisions, and open blockers.

Move process content out of reader-facing notes and into the relevant audit note before publication-ready claims.
