# Congregation Outreach Update Template

## 1. Spiritual opening

Begin with Scripture or a short spiritual frame.

Example:

> In Matthew 25, our Lord says, "I was hungry and you gave Me food; I was thirsty and you gave Me drink; I was a stranger and you took Me in." The Church has always heard this not as an idea, but as a commandment.

## 2. What this ministry is

Name the ministry plainly and describe the service in one or two sentences.

## 3. Why it matters

Explain that the ministry is about mercy, dignity, prayer, and obedience to Christ. Avoid branding language.

## 4. What is happening now

Include concrete details:

- next service date
- meeting/departure time
- preparation needed
- training completed
- approved budget or supply needs
- partner organizations, if any

## 5. How the congregation can help

- Pray
- Give
- Prepare supplies or food
- Join the service
- Help organize or communicate

Include the line when fitting:

> Not everyone has to go in person to be part of the service. Some will pray, some will give, some will prepare, and some will go. All of that matters.

## 6. Closing invitation

End with confidence and clarity.

Example:

> The direction is clear. The invitation is open. Come be part of it.
